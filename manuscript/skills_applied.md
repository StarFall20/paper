# Applied paper revision skills

This project applies two public skills from Adkid-Zephyr as editorial guidance.

## Oral Paper Skill

Source: https://github.com/Adkid-Zephyr/oral-paper-skill

Applied practices:

1. **Research tension:** the unresolved decision is when flexible prediction adds value after a choice model has represented the relevant utility structure.
2. **Contribution delta:** the paper changes the output of ML from a competing black-box predictor to a diagnostic that proposes utility terms for behavioural refitting.
3. **Claim-evidence alignment:** predictive scores, parameter recovery, WTP recovery, calibration, and decision regret are reported as distinct outcomes.
4. **Meaningful comparison:** all models use the same respondent-level split, candidate terms are selected inside nested validation, and the decision unit is the respondent-task choice.
5. **Conditions beside conclusions:** every result is tied to a stated mechanism condition, sample size, replication count, and validation regime.
6. **Bounded lesson:** the study supports a model-selection rule for the tested simulation and empirical datasets; it does not establish a universal complexity threshold.

## Anti-Defensive Writing

Source: https://github.com/Adkid-Zephyr/anti-defensive-writing-Skill

Applied rules:

- Organize the manuscript around the strongest supported contribution.
- Give each experiment one argumentative duty.
- State the advantage and its operating conditions directly.
- Remove process chronology, self-weakening phrases, and repeated caveats.
- Keep load-bearing scope conditions in the methods, results, and one focused limitations paragraph.
- Keep the abstract and conclusion centered on the contribution and evidence.

These rules guide presentation. They do not change reported results or remove necessary methodological qualifications.
