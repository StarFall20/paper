## Current judgement

The paper has a credible JOCM methods submission threshold only when framed as a
finite-support **design certificate and support-repair rule**. The score is not
presented as a new general lack-of-fit statistic: its finite-support inferential
boundary matches the saturated fibre alternative. The closest methodological
collision is exposure-mapping specification testing in network interference;
the DCE-specific increment is complete-menu-preserving fibres and a pre-outcome
rank/eigenvalue certificate.
