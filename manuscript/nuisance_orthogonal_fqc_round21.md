# Nuisance-orthogonal fibre quotient completeness: innovation audit

## Decision

The strongest independent contribution is **nuisance-orthogonal fibre
quotient completeness (NO-FQC)**. Fibre quotient completeness is its
zero-nuisance special case. The upgrade makes the paper address the main
identification objection to a reflected-task test: a rejection can arise from
an omitted utility direction, a scale change, a framing shift, or a task
processing response. NO-FQC defines the utility signal only after projecting
away from a declared local nuisance tangent space.

The claim is deliberately local. NO-FQC is a design-based certificate for
testability of a declared departure space under a declared nuisance library.
It is not a universal misspecification test, a general semiparametric
efficiency theorem, or a new generic maximin criterion.

## Idea DNA

The generation used two heterogeneous inferred parents and one inversion.

| locus | candidate |
|---|---|
| scientific target | candidate-basis sufficiency in a discrete-choice experiment |
| parent A object | candidate-preserving fibre and odd contrast operator |
| parent B object | nuisance-tangent projection and orthogonal score from semiparametric inference |
| mutation | reverse the usual order: design the falsification contrast before outcomes, then remove declared nuisance directions before ranking tasks |
| estimand | residualized operator rank, residual information eigenvalues, and the smallest support repair |
| validation | omitted decomposition versus scale, framing, unlisted complexity, and geometry-dependent presentation |
| hard locks | full menu vector preservation, respondent-level assignment, declared local departure space, reproducible task pool |
| stop rule | if residual rank is zero or the nuisance library is not frozen, the paper must report the direction as untestable |

The two parents answer different questions. The fibre construction removes the
candidate utility relation for every coefficient vector. Orthogonal projection
removes first-order sensitivity to declared nuisance directions. Their
combination yields one residual testability operator rather than two adjacent
tests.

## The single new object

Let D_S be the task-by-departure matrix generated by the odd fibre contrast.
Let G_S collect local nuisance tangents for signed framing/order effects,
geometry-dependent error scale, and any other nuisance mechanism frozen before
outcomes. Let W be the inverse covariance metric of the task contrasts. Set

    P_N = G_S (G_S' W G_S)^(-1) G_S' W

and

    D_S^perp = (I - P_N) D_S.

The NO-FQC rank is rank(D_S^perp). The full-support benchmark is
rank(D_X^perp). A block is complete when its residual rank reaches the
full-support rank. The local information matrix is

    K_S = D_S' W (I-P_N) D_S.

Its smallest eigenvalue is a conditioning measure after nuisance removal. A
zero residual column means that the departure is indistinguishable from the
declared nuisance span on the available support. A zero full-support column
means that the attribute support cannot test that departure at all.

For a single target direction g, the optimal unit-variance contrast is

    w* = W(I-P_N)g / sqrt(g'W(I-P_N)g).

It satisfies G_S' w* = 0 and retains the largest first-order target signal
among all contrasts orthogonal to the nuisance tangent space.

## Why this is a real methodological increment

Ordinary efficient DCE design maximizes precision for a chosen model.
Model-robust maximin design protects efficiency across model classes. Standard
misspecification checks operate on the support that happens to be observed.
NO-FQC asks a different design question: after the candidate relation is
imposed and the declared nuisance span is removed, how many utility departures
remain testable and how well conditioned is the weakest one?

The method transfer is substantive. Nuisance-orthogonal scores are common in
semiparametric inference, while candidate-preserving reflected task
construction is specific to the choice experiment. The projection is useful
only because the fibre design gives a coefficient-robust utility cancellation
and a respondent-cluster randomization reference. Removing either component
changes the estimand.

NO-FQC survives because its estimand is a residual rank and information
geometry, not predictive accuracy, model-class efficiency, or an interval of
policy outputs.

## Controlled evidence

The generic two-coordinate benchmark uses 200 replications, 600 respondents,
and 499 respondent-level sign flips. Rejection rates for the
nuisance-orthogonal contrast are .035 under the null, .755 for the declared
decomposition departure, .025 for declared scale, .040 for framing, .040 for
unlisted complexity, and .390 for the combined departure. The raw-signal
comparator gives .025, 1.000, .030, .035, .095, and .860. The unlisted
geometry-framing condition rejects at .980 for NO-FQC, documenting the boundary
of a finite nuisance library.

The smart-device structural benchmark removes exact squared-distance matching
so that geometry scale can vary inside the fibre design. It enumerates 136,179
reflections and selects one task from each candidate-gap bin. The NO-FQC block
has information eigenvalues 5.745, 6.909, and 11.492 after projection; the
matched raw-signal block has 0.172, 0.344, and 1.274. In 200 replications with
600 respondents, the NO-FQC and raw-signal rejection rates are:

| condition | NO-FQC | raw signal |
|---|---:|---:|
| null | .070 | .045 |
| omitted decomposition | .375 | .755 |
| declared scale | .035 | .085 |
| combined | .060 | .075 |
| signed framing | .045 | .215 |
| unlisted complexity | .055 | .095 |
| geometry framing outside the library | .995 | 1.000 |

The power cost is visible: projecting nuisance directions reduces sensitivity
to a departure aligned with them. That trade-off is part of the contribution
and must be reported. The method earns its value by keeping declared nuisance
false positives near size and by exposing when the library is incomplete.

The prespecified effect-strength check gives NO-FQC power .375 at
$\eta=0.10$ and .980 at $\eta=0.30$ for the omitted decomposition; the
raw-signal comparator gives .755 and 1.000. This curve reports the cost of
nuisance protection instead of hiding it in a single tuned effect size.

All values are planning simulations. They do not establish a human preference
effect.

## Literature boundary

The recent JOCM corpus emphasizes flexible specification, Bayesian and
sequential DCE design, varying choice-set size, scale and taste heterogeneity,
model-choice effects, and respondent engagement. Those papers establish the
importance of model uncertainty and task complexity. They do not provide a
candidate-preserving fibre operator residualized against nuisance tangents.

The nearest methodological precedents are:

- Fok and Paap, *New misspecification tests for multinomial logit models*,
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jocm.2024.100531
- Healy and Leo, *Which experiments test a model?*,
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jet.2026.106191
- Mao, Kessels, and van der Zanden, *Constructing Bayesian optimal designs
  for discrete choice experiments by simulated annealing*,
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jocm.2025.100551
- Quainoo et al., *Model choice and framing effects*,
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jocm.2024.100524
- Errore, Nachtsheim, and Li, *Maximin Model Robust Discrete Choice
  Experiments*, https://iris.unipa.it/handle/10447/117074
- Chernozhukov et al., *Double/debiased machine learning for treatment and
  structural parameters*, https://doi.org/10.1111/ectj.12097

The first four define the choice-modelling boundary and the fifth defines the
method-transfer boundary. The present paper must state that the projection
idea is transferred from nuisance-orthogonal inference; the DCE-specific
increment is the candidate-preserving fibre construction and its residual
rank certificate.

The available web and OpenAlex searches found no exact “nuisance-orthogonal
fibre” or “residualized fibre completeness” paper. Direct Scopus and Google
Scholar subscription exports remain required before a first-priority claim.

## Submission gate

The primary paper can be written around NO-FQC only if it adds:

1. a full proof of the local projection proposition and the multinomial
   covariance version;
2. a preregistered nuisance library, with one deliberately unlisted
   mechanism;
3. a paired DCE or external dataset carrying the candidate-preserving
   assignment;
4. a power calculation that reports the information loss from projection;
5. the support-repair certificate for directions with zero residual rank.

If the paired DCE cannot be collected, the correct claim is a
simulation-validated design method. The paper should not call the result a
human behavioural finding or a universal model test.
