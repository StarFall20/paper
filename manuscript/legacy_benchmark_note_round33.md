# Legacy benchmark note

`support_complete_vs_saturated_benchmark_round32_n400_r1000.csv` and `support_complete_vs_saturated_benchmark_round32_n800.csv` compare the proposed score with a saturated profile likelihood against a known simulated baseline probability. They are retained for provenance. They are not the unrestricted conditional-sufficiency LR used in the primary round-33 comparator. The primary comparator is `results/fibre_baseline_comparison.csv`, generated by `analysis/fibre_baseline_comparison.py`.
