# Power curve round 33

The power grid uses 100 replications per cell and 199 conditional-randomization draws. The null condition supplies the calibration reference; the hidden-orthogonal condition varies departure amplitude eta and respondent count.

| n | eta 0.10 | eta 0.20 | eta 0.30 | eta 0.45 |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 200 | 0.22 | 0.55 | 0.89 | 1.00 |
| 400 | 0.35 | 0.94 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| 800 | 0.47 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |

Entries are support-complete rejection rates. The complete-fibre LR rates are 0.22, 0.54, 0.92, 0.99 for n=200; 0.37, 0.93, 1.00, 1.00 for n=400; and 0.51, 1.00, 1.00, 1.00 for n=800. Null rejection rates for the support-complete/LR pair are 0.07/0.07 at n=200, 0.04/0.04 at n=400, and 0.02/0.01 at n=800 in this 100-replication calibration run.

The strong-signal benchmark is therefore a boundary check. The paper's evidence for practical power is the full eta-by-n curve, not a single 1.000 cell.
