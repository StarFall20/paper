# Suggested addition to Supplementary Appendix H

Both sample roles can contribute to the log partition function while task correlation changes existence. At a fixed correction weight of 0.90 and equal sample sizes, the critical ratio is 0.754 slopes per labelled respondent for independent tasks and 0.572 at within-respondent correlation 0.99. The corresponding geometric bounds place the second threshold below 0.635 and the first above 0.750. With 192 respondents in each role and 127 slopes, 96 of 100 independent-task fits and 9 of 100 correlated-task fits have finite minimizers. All 200 ordinary fits have finite minimizers. The task marginals and the population cluster sandwich covariance agree across the two designs. The forecast is fixed at one half and the correctly specified human choice probability is one half on every task.
