# Scope of the nearest mathematical and prediction correction results

The comparison separates the event already covered by general geometry from the explicit compensated profile and its statistical consequences. It records inspected statements, not a claim of exhaustive priority search.

Götze, Kabluchko and Zaporozhets, [Theorem3.1 and Lemma5.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1911.04184v2), already relate arbitrary Gaussian images to cone angles and relative interiors. Here set M equal to the scaled residual box intersected with the nuisance kernel. Conditional on the low-dimensional tuple, the remaining Gaussian matrix is independent of this set. Their absorption identity applies to this geometric step. The manuscript attributes it directly; multiplying intervals by a known scale supplies no new absorption principle.

Kabluchko and Seidel, [Theorem3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2006.09492v2), give a regular-cube Gaussian-zonotope expression. The constant-one-half, unit-power, fixed-intercept reduction in AppendixA6 remains explicitly attributed. The new result evaluates the corrected asymmetric profile under a fixed scale law, then derives the matched-covariance and auxiliary-ratio consequences. Neither the regular-cube reduction nor the Gaussian nullspace representation is claimed as the contribution.

[Tang and Ye](https://arxiv.org/html/1908.06208) already establish ordinary elliptical binary-response existence transitions. The present paired scale experiment leaves human separation exactly unchanged. Its object is the subtracted prediction-loss target; the ordinary result supplies a reference boundary, with the intercept convention stated explicitly.

[Koriyama and Bellec](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/koriyama25a.html) establish general Gaussian M-estimator existence theory. The inspected arXiv version requires finite absolute row infima. Corrected labelled rows here can be unbounded below. This prevents direct invocation of that theorem; the assumption difference alone does not establish independent novelty.

[Xu and Sun](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.10464), Section3.3, minimize filtered GLM loss over a compact coefficient set and derive covariance formulas. Harmful aggregation is established prior. The present unrestricted attainment event can fail with oracle predictions while full regular covariance improves; it makes no performance claim against their complete filtered procedure.

[Shoham et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.22586v1), Section3 and Algorithm1, use fresh batches for population stationarity. Their correction algebra and online variance tuning remain prior. The present construction concerns one frozen empirical objective, without an online algorithm comparison.

The candidate contribution is one consequence of the same compensated choice objective: explicit boundary evaluation shows that matching the full regular information and samplewise human separation can coexist with opposite attainment limits, and increasing auxiliary data along an existing variance rule can improve that covariance while removing attainment. The scale law is specified, bounded and fixed before the proportional limit. This is a derived result within established theory. Specific-result priority requires further literature assessment; numerical agreement and citation differences do not prove it.
