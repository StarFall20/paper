# Frequency path and local diagnostic checks

For each forecast, budget and correction weight with a verified crossing, take the first allocation with ordinary finite Logit and a crossing in the planned scenario order. Use the first scenario pair in that allocation. A linear program extends the finite endpoint toward the exterior endpoint and returns the exact last feasible transfer along that path, up to numerical tolerances. Recompute feasibility on each side and evaluate the recession cost at the returned boundary. Both endpoints and all intermediate weights retain the original menus.

Run a separate exploratory check from the finite endpoint. Use the exterior partner's recorded recession direction. Minimize the recession cost in that direction subject to positive auxiliary frequencies, total auxiliary mass one, and matching the original zero-point score and full Hessian. Each row has between one quarter and four times its starting weight. Test total variation budgets 0.20 and 0.60. Record every direction and case tested, including inconclusive and finite cases. This direction-based construction measures whether such perturbations exist in the released menu library. The number found cannot estimate how often they arise in routine designs.
