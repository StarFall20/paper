# Closest current claims on oracle prediction

| Primary source | Verified comparison |
| --- | --- |
| [Angelopoulos, Duchi and Zrnic, PPI++ v2, Corollary 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.01453v2#S3) | Derives regular asymptotic inference under nonsingular limiting information and the stated limit for tuning. Matching derivatives through any finite order at the true parameter does not establish attainment on a particular realised menu design. |
| [Chen, Bai and Zhu, Covariance-Calibrated Prediction-Powered Inference v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.21837v2#S4) | Compares regular estimators and conditions its structured logistic risk statement on a finite pilot. The common-menu construction gives different attainment outcomes for the same corrected criterion, with a common correctly specified informative predictor. |
| [Xu and Sun, Filtered Prediction-Powered Inference v1, Theorem 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.10464v1) | The strong separation condition concerns learning a region defined by gaps from an oracle regression function. That theorem does not classify existence of a choice likelihood minimizer. |

The comparison uses the author-supplied PPI++ v2 with its embedded appendices and the current author preprints. Project Euclid's separate final supplements remain unverified. The Scopus session was unavailable on reconnect.
