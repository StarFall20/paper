# Relation to published results

Existence of an unpenalized estimate as an interior support condition and failure along directions of recession are classical. The study of ordinary logistic separation in D-optimal designs also predates this work. The calculation here decomposes the recession of the same compensated choice logit into observed choice regret and a difference between two prediction-centered design supports. It gives an exact, power-independent criterion over all response configurations and a sharp conservative power for fixed observed responses under the stated auxiliary-design class. [Geyer (2009)](https://www.stat.umn.edu/geyer/gdor/) [Park, Mancenido and Montgomery (2019)](https://experts.azregents.edu/en/publications/separation-in-d-optimal-experimental-designs-for-the-logistic-reg/) [PPI++ v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.01453v2)

Containment by support functions is established convex analysis. Sadraddini and Tedrake give computational encodings for containment of affine images of polytopes. This work uses only directional witnesses for possible failure and explicitly leaves a no-witness result unresolved. It applies separate moment-support calculations to the already observed responses. [Sadraddini and Tedrake (2019)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.05214)

Diaconis–Ylvisaker conjugate penalization produces finite estimates using a positive pseudo-response mixture. Its interior moment argument supplies a direct analogue to the safety bound. The compensated objective here retains the observed-minus-predicted correction. The mixture is a certificate about the labelled moment and is absent from the fitted objective. [Rigon and Aliverti (2023)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167715223001256)
