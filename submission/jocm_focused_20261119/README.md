The main result connects fixed choice menus and prediction correction to whether a finite logit estimate exists. A 32-plus-32 row example holds the predictor, weight, labelled choices and sample sizes fixed, with identical local derivatives through third order and three distinct existence outcomes. The general construction matches any specified finite order. Independent checks used exact arithmetic on 30 cases up to order 33 and original menus on 1,960 Swissmetro fit points and 7,536 food-choice fit points. The current verification recomputes checks from the public data and reconciles archived Monte Carlo outputs with the reported tables.

[Result-level literature comparison](literature_update.md) records the narrow originality claim and database coverage. The applications use public choice-study archives. The retained allocations share underlying respondents, and the reported comparisons describe those allocations. The positive theoretical results apply under the stated choice and feature laws. Final author confirmation applies to the submission files and declarations.
