# Synthetic regularization and vanishing-power comparison

10 November2026. The comparison uses primary papers,with the inspected versions and units stated below. It is a bounded mathematical comparison,not a completed Scopus/Scholar review.

[Li and Huang2026,arxiv2407.04194v5](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.04194v5),Section2.1 equations4–5,studies a positively weighted sum of observed and synthetic log likelihoods. Section3.1 Theorem1 gives existence under nonseparation and full rank of the synthetic design. Sections3–4 develop broader stability and proportional-dimension theory. This is direct prior for synthetic data regularization and high-dimensional inference. It rules out presenting regularization by predicted/synthetic data,or obtaining a finite fit after human separation,as an innovation by itself.

For a proper binary target t∈[0,1],the row logistic recession cost is (u)_+−tu≥0. Thus H+cB with fixed proper labels has the same zero recession directions for every c>0:the intersection of the two nonnegative zero sets. Its positive-weight design rank is also invariant. The finite-root event is weight invariant. For Q_λ=(1−λ)H+λA,the corrected A subtracts labelled prediction loss and can have negative recession cost. The compensation and the finite-root event change with λ. This algebra is our direct comparison of the two specified losses;it is not an attributed claim that Li–Huang studied the corrected objective.

Its proof uses the same vanishing-loss mechanism and a bounded auxiliary softplus remainder. The specific cost and log(1/λ) scaling are made explicit;this is not claimed as a new general theory of margin maximization.
