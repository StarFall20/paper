## Current version of the closest general existence theorem

[Koriyama and Bellec's arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03163) identifies v3, revised 25 May 2025, as the current version. Its [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.03163), printed page 3, Assumption 2.5(2), requires integrability of the absolute rowwise infimum. Assumption 2.5(1) also requires strictly convex individual losses. Theorem 2.6 on printed page 4 gives their existence transition under the working assumptions. This confirms that the finite-infimum restriction retained in the manuscript's explicitly versioned v2 citation persists in v3. Unit-power labelled rows are affine and also fail strict convexity. These observations establish lack of direct coverage by that theorem. They do not by themselves establish originality of the compensated boundary.

The closest-prior assessment remains at the level of the specified compensated profile and its consequences. Logistic separation, Gaussian conic geometry, moment-support criteria and margin limits remain established foundations. No general separation or conic principle is claimed as new.
