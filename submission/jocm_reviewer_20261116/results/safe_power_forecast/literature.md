# Scope of the predictive comparison

PPI++ supplies the compensated estimator and a variance-based power rule. Mani et al. give exact finite-sample analysis for estimation of a population mean, including correlations needed for improvement. The two choice datasets here assess choice outcomes at fixed menus and separate existence, coefficient fitting and held-out probability loss. The mean-estimation theorem and this choice-forecast comparison address distinct targets. [PPI++ preprint and appendices](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.01453v2) [Mani et al., ICML 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/mani26b.html)

Ye and Yoganarasimhan allocate fixed human budgets across LLM-augmented survey estimation tasks using rectification difficulty and include a multinomial logit application. In this audit auxiliary subsamples vary at fixed labels and a given fitted choice specification. The direct MNL comparison makes every training choice available to the alternative estimator. The empirical comparison holds tasks and the archived training budget fixed. [Ye and Yoganarasimhan, v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17267v2)

The derivative is the implicit derivative of a familiar estimating equation. Its contribution here is diagnostic evidence on the same finite-estimation designs, with two explicit human cost comparisons. Evidence is observational within two fixed public choice cohorts.
